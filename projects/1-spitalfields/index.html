1 Spitalfields.

Large-scale architecture,
professional delivery.

At Foster + Partners, I contributed to 1 Spitalfields as the project developed from RIBA Stage 2 into Stage 3. My work combined architectural design, coordinated Revit modelling and drawing production with visual communication across the main building, market and affordable workspace.

Professional practice

Design, coordination
and communication.

I worked within a multidisciplinary team developing coordinated architectural information across the project. My responsibilities included design development, Revit model coordination and drawing packages, alongside work on the atrium, landscape, market planning and mezzanine canopy.

The project required the same information to operate at several levels: as a coordinated BIM model, as technical drawings used across the team, and as spatial visualisations for design review and communication. I also translated project models into Unreal Engine environments and film-based outputs to make complex design information easier to understand.
